## 1. Information used on your device

NextMedi uses the medication names, forms, amounts, markers and colors you enter; schedule times, intervals, repetitions, periods and limits; recorded doses, skipped doses, cancellations and corrections; and display, language and visibility preferences. This information supports schedule calculations, reminders, widgets, history and backups. Medication records may contain sensitive health information.

Medication and schedule records are stored locally. The app does not provide accounts, synchronization to an operator server, first-party analytics or automatic crash reporting. Personal World does not receive your medication database through these features or sell your medication records.

## 2. Retention, protection and deletion

Archiving or ending a schedule retains its past records. Canceling or correcting a dose also preserves the history. To delete the local database and settings, clear NextMedi's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. The app excludes its data from automatic Android cloud backup and automatic device transfer.

There is no additional database encryption. Protection relies on Android's app storage isolation and your device's screen lock. Keep your device protected.

## 3. Backups, visible information and diagnostics

You can export an unencrypted JSON backup to a location selected through Android's file picker. It contains medication details and record history. If you choose a cloud provider, that provider may receive and store the file under its own terms. Exported files remain after uninstalling NextMedi; delete them separately from the chosen location. Imports merge with existing data and reject conflicting records.

Medication names and doses may appear in the app, notifications and widgets. Names are visible on a new installation. Settings let you independently hide names and doses in home widgets, lock-screen widgets and lock-screen notifications. Existing choices are preserved. Widget shapes and colors remain visible when names are hidden. Other people who can see your screen may see the displayed information. Device lock-screen settings also affect visibility.

If data cannot be read, you may save a diagnostic report containing only the app version, Android API version and a fixed error category. It does not include medication names, dose times, device identifiers or exception logs. The app does not send this report automatically.

## 4. Third-party SDKs and network processing

The current release configuration disables the app's advertising display and ad-request features. Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP), including advertising-related permissions, remain included in the app package.

Google describes SDK processing that can include IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, depending on SDK configuration and operation. This does not mean every listed category is transmitted by this release; disabling the app's ad requests is also not a claim that every SDK operation is blocked. Google controls its own processing and retention under its policies. The app does not include medication names, dose amounts or dose times in ad-request parameters.

In builds where advertising is enabled, the app uses UMP consent information before requesting ads. Available ad privacy choices can be reopened in Settings. Android settings may allow resetting or deleting the advertising ID.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## 5. Permissions

Notification permission displays schedule reminders. Exact-alarm access schedules reminders for the times you enter. Boot completion allows the app to rebuild valid reminders after a restart. Denying reminder permissions does not prevent creating schedules or recording doses, but reminders may be limited or delayed.

## 6. Support requests and changes

If you email Personal World, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to reply and resolve the issue. Please do not include unnecessary medication or other sensitive details. We retain support information while needed to handle the request and delete it when no longer needed, except where a legal retention obligation applies.

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete information you have sent to us, contact **j12i29j12i29@gmail.com**. Personal World cannot remotely retrieve or delete the medication database stored only on your device; use the deletion steps above for that data.
